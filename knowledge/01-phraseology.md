# ATC Phraseology

ATC transmissions should use concise aviation radio phraseology.

## General principles

Use short, unambiguous transmissions.

Prefer standard aviation terminology over conversational language.

Do not add explanations, commentary, greetings, or unnecessary words.

The controller should communicate only information or instructions that are operationally relevant.

## Common instructions

Use standard forms such as:

- "Cleared for takeoff runway 23."
- "Hold short runway 23."
- "Taxi to runway 23."
- "Maintain runway heading."
- "Climb and maintain 3000 feet."
- "Report downwind."
- "Report base."
- "Report final."
- "Cleared to land runway 23."
- "Go around."

Do not embellish transmissions with phrases such as:

- "Okay, let's..."
- "I want you to..."
- "Please..."
- "You are good to go..."
- explanations of why an instruction is being issued.

## Clearances vs instructions

A clearance authorizes an operation.

Examples:

- "Cleared for takeoff runway 23."
- "Cleared to land runway 23."

An instruction directs the aircraft.

Examples:

- "Maintain runway heading."
- "Climb and maintain 2000 feet."
- "Report downwind."

Do not invent operational information such as wind, QNH, frequencies, traffic, headings, altitudes, or clearances unless that information is available from the simulation state or established situation.

## Takeoff clearance

"Cleared for takeoff runway xx" authorizes the aircraft to begin its takeoff.

A takeoff clearance is different from:

"Taxi to runway xx"

which authorizes taxiing but not takeoff.

After a takeoff clearance has been correctly read back, ATC should not issue the same takeoff clearance again unless circumstances have changed.

## Initial climb

After takeoff, the aircraft enters its initial climb.

ATC may issue instructions concerning:

- heading
- altitude
- reporting points
- traffic-pattern entry

The aircraft's current telemetry determines whether it has actually taken off and whether it is climbing.
